AI Mode goes further than AI Overviews: instead of summarizing above the blue links, it replaces the results page with a generated, multi-turn answer experience. It leans heavily on query fan-out, breaking your question into many sub-queries behind the scenes.

It rolled out across Google Search in 2025 and is free to use. For visibility, the implication is the same as the rest of GEO: be reachable, be the clearest extractable answer, and be corroborated across trusted sources.
