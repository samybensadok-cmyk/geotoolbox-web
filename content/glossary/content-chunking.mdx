Because retrieval works at the passage level, dangling references and buried answers hurt you. Each section should open with its point and hold together if lifted out of context.

Good chunking overlaps with answer-first writing: a clear heading, a direct answer underneath, then supporting detail.

One caution on the word itself: Google's generative-AI guide lists "chunking content" (pre-fragmenting pages to hit chunk-size specs) among the tactics to skip. The durable part is passages that stand alone, not the folklore numbers.
