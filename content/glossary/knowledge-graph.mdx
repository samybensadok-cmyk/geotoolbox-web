Search and AI engines reason about entities, not just strings of text. The scale is hard to overstate: Google's graph held over 500 billion facts about five billion entities by its last public figure, from May 2020. If your brand is a well-defined, consistently described entity across the web, the model can connect facts to you with confidence.

Entity clarity, structured data, and consistent descriptions across trusted sources all feed how an engine places you in its knowledge graph.

The same graph idea is showing up in agent tooling. Google's [Open Knowledge Format](https://geotoolbox.ai/blog/okf-vs-rag) packages curated concepts as cross-linked Markdown files an AI agent can walk, a lightweight knowledge graph you maintain yourself.
