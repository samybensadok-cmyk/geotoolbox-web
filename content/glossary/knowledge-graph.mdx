Search and AI engines reason about entities, not just strings of text. If your brand is a well-defined, consistently described entity across the web, the model can connect facts to you with confidence.

Entity clarity, structured data, and consistent descriptions across trusted sources all feed how an engine places you in its knowledge graph.
