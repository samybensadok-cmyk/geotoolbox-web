GEO, AEO, LLM SEO, and AI search engine optimization are largely interchangeable. Picking a label is a marketing choice, not a strategy.

The work behind all of them is the same: make sure AI crawlers can reach your pages, structure answers so they are easy to extract, and earn consistent mentions across trusted sources. The full guide breaks it down.
