Unlike a backlink, a brand mention does not need a hyperlink to carry weight. An engine reading consistent descriptions of your brand on Wikipedia, Reddit, news, and industry sites gains evidence that your facts are real and widely agreed upon.

This is why off-site presence matters as much as on-site optimization. A fact that only lives on your own domain is weaker than one echoed across several independent places.
