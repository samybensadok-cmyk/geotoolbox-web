AEO grew out of the featured-snippet and voice-search era and leans toward concise, direct answers. GEO is the newer label for the same instinct applied to generative engines that synthesize rather than excerpt.

Do not staff AEO and GEO as separate budgets. The underlying work, reachable and clearly-answered content, is shared. See the full guide for how the terms relate.
