AI engines reason about entities, not just text. Stating plainly who you are and what you do, and being described consistently across the web, strengthens your entity and your odds of being cited accurately.

Entity SEO feeds the knowledge graph: the clearer and more consistent your entity, the more confidently an engine can attribute a claim to you.
