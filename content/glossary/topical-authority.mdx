You build topical authority by covering a subject thoroughly: a pillar page on the core topic and supporting pages on its sub-questions, linked together so the relationship is clear. That internal structure tells engines the pieces belong to one entity and one area of expertise.

Authority is what turns recognition into citation. An engine that sees you own a subject is more likely to reach for you when someone asks about it.
