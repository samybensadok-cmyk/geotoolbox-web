Citation logic is not the same as ranking logic. Engines cite sources that are clear, current, corroborated, and easy to extract, which is why a high-authority page can be ignored while a sharper one is quoted.

You track citations by querying the engines for your topics and counting how often you appear versus competitors, which rolls up into your AI share of voice.
