The LLM SEO framing is useful for remembering that you are optimizing for how a model retrieves and synthesizes, not just how a search index ranks. But the tactics do not differ from GEO.

Reachability, answer-first structure, citable facts, and off-site corroboration are the levers, whatever you call the discipline.
