SGE began as a Search Labs experiment in 2023, generating a summarized answer above the classic results. After testing, Google rebranded it and rolled it out as **AI Overviews** for general availability, starting in the US in May 2024.

If you still see "SGE" in older guides, read it as AI Overviews. The optimization goal is unchanged: be one of the sources the overview cites, which depends on clear, helpful content rather than any special markup.
