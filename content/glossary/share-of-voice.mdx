Because AI answers vary, share of voice is measured by sampling: run your core questions through the engines repeatedly and track the proportion in which you show up.

Some tools also weigh sentiment, not just frequency, since a negative mention is not the same as a recommendation. It is directional, not exact, but the trend tells you whether your GEO work is moving you into more answers over time.
