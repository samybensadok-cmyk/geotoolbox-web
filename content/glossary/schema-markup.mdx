Treat schema as housekeeping that aids parsing, not a growth hack. Types like Article, FAQPage, Organization, and DefinedTerm help engines disambiguate your pages and brand.

Clarity in the prose still does more than markup. Schema supports machine understanding; it does not substitute for being the clearest answer.
