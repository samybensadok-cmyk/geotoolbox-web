Perplexity vs Claude is mostly a question about jobs, not quality. Perplexity is a search product that cites its sources and lets paid users pick the model, Claude included. Claude is Anthropic's own assistant, with web search built in. On the entry plans, which cost $17 a month billed annually, Perplexity Pro's Search model list has Claude Sonnet 5.5 only, while Claude Pro includes Opus and Claude Code. If you want sourced answers, Perplexity is built for that. If you write, analyze documents or code, Claude is the better single subscription.

Most of the 6 comparison articles we analyzed (the top-ranking ones, run through NotebookLM) frame the difference as retrieval versus reasoning. Perplexity pulls pages in real time and cites them. ## Does Perplexity Use Claude?

Yes, on paid plans. Perplexity says it orchestrates models from several vendors, Anthropic's Claude among them, and that [Pro and Max both allow selecting a preferred model](https://www.perplexity.ai/hub/pricing). Which Claude you can pick depends on the plan. Perplexity's [table of Search models, dated October 27, 2026 and checked November 7](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/10354919-what-advanced-ai-models-are-included-in-my-subscription), lists Claude Sonnet 5.5 on Pro and Max, and Claude Opus 5.5 and Claude Fable 5.1 on Max only.

[Claude Pro](https://claude.com/pricing) includes Opus and offers Fable through usage credits (from November 9 through November 16, 2026, the Fable 5.2 launch offer includes it up to half the weekly limit). So if Opus is the reason you want Claude, it costs $17 to $20 a month on Claude Pro. On Perplexity it means Max, at $167 a month billed annually or $200 monthly. The trade runs both ways: Perplexity Pro's Search list also includes models Claude Pro does not offer, such as GPT-6.1 Sol, Gemini 3.8 Flash and Grok 4.7.

### Best Picks the Model Unless You Do

Perplexity's help page says Best "picks a suitable model for each query", so Claude answers only when you choose it or Best happens to pick it. If you are paying for Claude inside Perplexity, set the model yourself.

### The Table Covers Search, and It Changes

That table is for Search. Perplexity says Computer has [its own model selector and availability rules](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/10354919-what-advanced-ai-models-are-included-in-my-subscription), and Comet has its own model selection too, so a Pro subscriber may reach other models there. Perplexity also dates its list and says it changes as providers release and retire models, so check the model menu in your own account.

## Research and Citations: Which One Gets Sources Right?

Perplexity is built around search, and its pricing page says [every answer includes inline citations](https://www.perplexity.ai/hub/pricing). Claude has web search on every plan, Free included, but in its merged chat and Cowork experience there is [no web search toggle: Claude searches the web when it helps](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/16761823-claude-cowork-and-chat-are-one-claude). For anything recent, tell Claude to search and cite.

### What the Accuracy Studies Cover

We found no independent study that measures claim-level citation accuracy for both Claude and Perplexity (we searched on November 7), and the ones that exist cover different systems. One [2025 arXiv study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.18059) ran 8 chatbots, Claude and Perplexity among them, through the same academic-reference prompt, but it checked whether the references were real and correct, not whether web-search citations support claims. The [Tow Center's March 2025 test](https://www.cjr.org/tow_center/we-compared-eight-ai-search-engines-theyre-all-bad-at-citing-news.php) of 8 AI search tools found Perplexity answered 37 percent of its news queries incorrectly, against 94 percent for Grok 3. It did not test Claude. The [DeepTRACE audit](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.04499), posted to arXiv in September 2025, scored Perplexity's search mode at 31.6% unsupported statements and 49.0% citation accuracy, and its Deep Research mode at 97.5% unsupported and 58.0%. In the same table GPT 4.5 scored 39.8%, so Perplexity was not the outlier. A May 2026 arXiv study, [Cited but Not Verified](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06635), tested Claude but no Perplexity system: its fact-check dimension scored Claude Opus 4.5 at 76.8%, Claude Haiku 4.5 at 68.9% and Claude Sonnet 4.6 at 58.7%. All of these predate today's models and use different methods, so you cannot subtract one result from another.

### Human Preference Favors Claude with Search, on One Board

On [Arena's Search leaderboard](https://arena.ai/leaderboard/chat/search), dated November 6, 2026 (1,537,892 votes, 42 models), Claude Opus 4.6 with search ranks #3 at 1252 and Claude Sonnet 4.6 with search #12 at 1220. Newer Claude entries also rank above both Sonar entries: Claude Opus 5 (High) is #5 at 1240, Claude Fable 5.1 (High) #6 at 1239 and Claude Opus 5.5 (Medium) #14 at 1214. Perplexity's Sonar Reasoning Pro (High) ranks #37 at 1139 and Sonar Pro (High) #39 at 1130. Voters pick the answer they prefer, not the one whose citations hold up, the entries are model-plus-search stacks rather than subscriptions, and a Perplexity Pro subscriber can run Claude Sonnet 5.5 inside Perplexity.

### We Ran the Same Research Questions Through Both

In geotoolbox's test on November 7, 2026, we put 5 questions to each engine. For 4 of them we had answers already pinned to vendor pages and the paper itself: the Max 20x rename, which Claude models Perplexity Pro includes, whether each vendor's user-triggered fetcher obeys robots.txt, and what DeepTRACE found about Perplexity. The fifth asked whether an independent study compares their web-search citation accuracy head to head, which tests whether an engine will invent one. Perplexity ran as Sonar Pro with web search, called through a data provider's API (DataForSEO) rather than Perplexity's own. [Perplexity says](https://docs.perplexity.ai/docs/agent-api/migrate-from-sonar/overview) support for its Sonar chat-completions API ended on September 27, 2026 and requests are being reformulated as Agent API requests, model by model. We cannot tell whether this call went through Sonar chat completions or the Agent API, so it may not match the engine behind today's app. Claude ran as Opus 5.5 with web search and page fetching. A separate Claude agent, shown the answers labeled A and B in shuffled order, scored each 0 to 2 against the known answers and opened the cited pages.

On 4 of the 5 questions both engines met the rubric's required facts. Perplexity missed the DeepTRACE figure and scored 0 of 2 on it, so the totals were 8 of 10 against Claude's 10 of 10. The rubric scored required facts only, so Claude's extra wrong detail cost it nothing. On sourcing, the grader preferred Claude's answer on 3 of the 4 questions both got right, because it cited the vendor pages and papers themselves, and called the rename question a tie. Speed went the other way: Perplexity answered in about 2 to 3 seconds per question and all 5 calls cost under 5 cents, while the Claude agent took about 3 minutes in total, with 13 searches and 12 page fetches, on subscription quota with no per-call price.

The DeepTRACE miss is the one to remember. A fast, cited answer attached 10 links, and the grader, who checked 4 of the links, found none that supported the figure it gave.

Treat this as a log, not a ranking. It is 5 questions, run once. The Claude agent and the grader come from the same model family as the assistant that drafted this article, the A and B labels hid the engine names but not their answer styles, and the engines had different tools: Claude's run visibly fetched and read full pages, while the API returned only Sonar Pro's answer and its links. Sonar Pro is also not the Claude Sonnet 5.5 that a Perplexity Pro subscriber can pick. What it does show is that both engines made a mistake you would only catch by opening the sources.

## Writing, Long Documents and Context Window

Most of those 6 articles give long-form writing to Claude. That is a shared opinion, not a measured result, but it fits how the products are built: Perplexity answers from retrieved pages, while Claude is a general assistant you can give a draft, a contract or a codebase. [Claude's pricing page](https://claude.com/pricing) lists a context window of up to 1M tokens on every plan, varying by model, and Perplexity's pricing page lists none. For long-form writing that has to hold one argument across thousands of words, start in Claude and bring your sources in. For a short, sourced briefing note, Perplexity's retrieve-then-write flow is built for that.

## Coding and Agents: Claude Code and Cowork vs Perplexity Computer

[Claude's pricing page](https://claude.com/pricing) lists Claude Code on Pro, Max and Ultra and not on Free, and [Anthropic's Ultra page](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/17728952-what-is-the-ultra-plan) lists it for terminal-based coding workflows on one subscription. Our guide to [what Claude Code is](https://geotoolbox.ai/blog/what-is-claude-code) covers how it works.

Perplexity's Computer is a different kind of tool. Its [models page](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/10354919-what-advanced-ai-models-are-included-in-my-subscription) calls it an autonomous agent that can research, code, create documents and use connected tools, and its [credits page](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/13838041-how-credits-work-on-perplexity) prices work per task, listing an entire app or multi-day build at 15,000 or more credits.

Claude Cowork, Claude's agent for longer tasks like research and reports, is changing. Anthropic says chat and Cowork are [merged into a single conversation](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/16761823-claude-cowork-and-chat-are-one-claude), rolling out in stages starting with Pro, Max and Ultra, so accounts on the same plan may not see it on the same day. If a comparison treats Claude Cowork as a separate product, check its date.

- **Coding:** Claude Code comes with the plan and draws on its usage limits. Computer can build apps too, but it bills by the task.
- **Research and report tasks:** compare Computer's credit cost with the usage your Claude plan includes.

Anthropic says its bots [respect "do not crawl" signals by honoring industry standard directives in robots.txt](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/8896518-does-anthropic-crawl-data-from-the-web-and-how-can-site-owners-block-the-crawler), and that a robots.txt rule naming ClaudeBot wins over a general setting. Perplexity's crawler documentation describes Perplexity-User as the one fetcher that generally ignores robots.txt. Perplexity's help center is less clear: [its robots.txt article, last modified September 14, 2026](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/10354969-how-does-perplexity-follow-robots-txt), says Perplexity respects robots.txt directives and that letting users summarize a blocked URL "has been disabled". The documentation page is newer and specific to the user-triggered fetcher, so we treat Perplexity-User as the exception, but do not rely on robots.txt alone: check your server logs for its user-agent string.

To stay citable in both, allow PerplexityBot, Claude-SearchBot and Claude-User. To opt out of Anthropic's training without leaving Claude's search results, restrict ClaudeBot only. Anthropic also says it has begun recognizing a machine-readable "no AI training" preference signal, rolling out over time, though a rule that names ClaudeBot still takes priority.

## Check These Before You Trust Any Comparison

### Check the Lineup Date

Perplexity's model table is dated October 27, 2026, and Claude's current lineup is Fable 5.2, Opus 5.5, Sonnet 5.5 and Haiku 5.5, while that table still lists Fable 5.1 and no Fable 5.2 as of November 9, 2026. Posts that say Perplexity Pro includes Opus in Search, or build their advice on Sonnet 4.6, do not match that table. Perplexity's own pricing page is inconsistent too: its FAQ says [Free "includes access to the top AI models"](https://www.perplexity.ai/hub/pricing) while the Free plan card says "basic AI models".

### Check the Plan Names

Anthropic's Ultra page, updated October 30, 2026, says Max 20x is now Ultra, and [Ultra is 10x Pro usage every week](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/17728952-what-is-the-ultra-plan). Any post that still sells Max 20x is describing a plan that now goes by another name.

### Check Whether the Product Still Exists as Described

Claude Cowork is being merged into Claude chat, and Perplexity's Computer is paid in credits that move with your plan. A comparison that treats either as fixed is a snapshot, and so is ours, which is why this page carries a re-check date of November 20.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Perplexity Pro worth paying for if you already have Claude Pro?

Only if you hunt for sources most days. Claude Pro already includes web search, so what Perplexity Pro adds is search built into each answer with inline citations, models from several vendors, and weekly Computer usage. If that describes your work, the second $17 plan earns its place. If not, stay on Claude Pro and use Perplexity's free tier when you need a sourced answer.

### Can Claude search the web like Perplexity?

Yes, on every plan, Free included. The difference is control: Perplexity is designed to search on each question, while in Claude's merged chat experience there is no toggle and Claude searches when it helps. For anything recent, ask Claude to search and to cite its sources.

### Claude or Perplexity: which is better for students?

Use both free tiers first. Perplexity is built for finding sources, and most comparisons favor Claude for working through a paper you have already downloaded. Whatever you cite in an essay, open the page: DeepTRACE measured 49.0% citation accuracy for Perplexity's search mode in 2025, and we found no independent study of claim-level citation accuracy for both.
