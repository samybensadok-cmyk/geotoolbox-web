## How We Chose These Agencies

We researched 22 agencies: the names that kept showing up in Google results, in AI answers and in rival lists, plus the agencies we found in gambling categories of the EGR B2B Awards, the European and Global Search Awards and the iGB Affiliate Awards. Some award-listed agencies could not be researched in time and are not counted: Serpact, and Basher Agency, BetAffiliation, Famesters and SEOBrothers from the iGB shortlist. Six passed. Each stage below uses public pages, and we ran them on November 15, 2026. Licenses change, so check again before you sign.

**1. An SEO-led agency with gambling evidence you can check.** Either a win or shortlist in a gambling category on the award organizer's own page, or a public case study naming a gambling client. A logo wall did not count, and neither did an "award-winning" badge we could not trace to the organizer. Media agencies, paid-search shops and affiliate program managers were out of scope. Nine agencies passed.

**2. Clients we could check.** We looked up every operator named in a case study, award entry or service page on an official list, mostly the [Gambling Commission's public register](https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register) in the UK and the operator lists of Oregon and Ontario in North America. Affiliates hold no operator license, so for affiliate clients we read the main offers page and checked a sample of the lesser-known operators on the register. Seven agencies passed.

**3. No disqualifiers.** We dropped any agency that sells private blog networks, promises rankings, or has an offshore or sweepstakes casino as a direct client. An affiliate client that promotes unlicensed operators is flagged under "Watch for" instead. In the end 6 agencies passed. Several agencies that rival lists rank near the top failed one of these stages, and we leave out their names.

One thing did not disqualify an agency: selling or pricing links. Four of the six describe per-link pricing, link placements or per-link billing on their own sites, which is normal in this market. Under [Google's spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies#link-spam), paying for a link that passes ranking credit is link spam, and so are links with optimized anchor text in articles, guest posts or press releases. Paid links qualified with `rel="sponsored"` or `rel="nofollow"` comply with that policy.

So each entry quotes the agency's own words on links under "Watch for". If you run a licensed brand, that is the first question to ask, and our guide to [casino SEO](https://geotoolbox.ai/blog/casino-seo) covers why.

### 1. Receptional: Best for UK-Licensed Operators

Receptional won the marketing services supplier category at the [EGR B2B Awards 2026](https://awards.egr.global/egrb2bawards/en/page/2026-winners), the gambling industry's own supplier awards. Founded in 1999, it now says iGaming is "all we do" and works across SEO, paid media, digital PR and conversion work.

Its [LiveScore Bet case study](https://www.receptional.com/casestudies/driving-high-value-casino-player-acquisition-for-livescorebet/) reports a 50% rise in non-brand clicks to the main casino page and page-one rankings within three months. A separate [Casumo campaign](https://www.receptional.com/casestudies/casumo/) claims 118 pieces of coverage and 57 branded backlinks. We found LiveScore Bet, Casumo's operator and Smarkets on the UK Gambling Commission register.

**Watch for:**
- Its [digital PR page](https://www.receptional.com/digital-pr-services/) offers "niche edits" and a database of sites that "will give you a dofollow link". Ask how each link is sourced and labeled.
- Its pages disagree on reach: the award post says "35+ regulated markets", the SEO page says "15 regulated markets". The digital PR page also credits Casumo with 168 backlinks, so ask which campaign each figure covers.

### 2. ICS-digital: Best for Multilingual, Multi-Market Operators

ICS-digital won the same EGR B2B category two years earlier, at the [EGR B2B Awards 2024](https://awards.egr.global/egrb2bawards/en/page/2024-winners). It is based in Leeds, has offered SEO since 2008 and belongs to Spotlight Sports Group, which also sells sports content and data to operators.

Its strength is languages. The agency says it works in 100 of them, and its [bet365 case study](https://www.ics-digital.com/case-studies/bet365) describes more than 10 years of on-page and off-site content for bet365 in 14 languages. Betfair is a second public case, built on content marketing around the FIFA World Cup. Both brands appear on the UK Gambling Commission register under their operating companies.

**Watch for:**
- Its [link building page](https://www.ics-digital.com/services/link-building-agency) says "Each link we offer has its own individual price". Ask what each price buys and whether the links are labeled.
- The bet365 case quotes bet365's overall audience, "approximately 250 million users" a month, rather than a result tied to the agency's work.
- Ask whether the Spotlight ownership affects the content it recommends.

### 3. Vega: Best for Sportsbook Digital PR

Vega won Best Use of Search, Gaming (Large) at the [European Search Awards 2025](https://europeansearchawards.com/2025-winners/) for its organic work with Rank Interactive, part of Rank Group. It runs offices in Manchester and Gibraltar, and its Gibraltar arm was on the [Global Search Awards 2026 shortlist](https://globalsearchawards.net/2026-shortlist/) with Lottoland.

That Lottoland campaign shows the method. Vega pitched sports experts to national sports desks around live news, and one interview with Guenther Steiner, run against live F1 news, produced 112 pieces of coverage. The [case study](https://www.vegagibraltar.com/case-studies/lottoland) claims non-brand sportsbook clicks rose 120% in summer 2026 against summer 2025. Rank Interactive and Lottoland's operator are both on the UK Gambling Commission register.

**Watch for:**
- The same page says the PR was "bought on a cost-per-link model, pointed at commercial pages with optimised anchors", and that every activation "was tracked like a link buy". That appears to describe how the client paid Vega rather than payments to newspapers, but Google's spam policies name optimized anchors in articles as a link spam pattern. Ask which anchors the agency plans for you.

### 4. Fortis Media: Best for US Horse Racing Betting

Fortis Media has the only named US operator case study among the six. Its [TwinSpires case study](https://www.fortismedia.com/en/articles/twinspires-fortis-media-interview-seo-partnership/) is an interview with TwinSpires' director of content and SEO, who credits the agency with growing share of voice and conversions. TwinSpires is Churchill Downs' horse racing betting platform, listed as an advance-deposit wagering hub by the [Oregon Racing Commission](https://www.oregon.gov/racing/Pages/Advance-Deposit-Wagering.aspx).

The agency lists addresses in Vilnius, Dublin and Wilmington, Delaware. Its [iGaming link building page](https://www.fortismedia.com/en/services/igaming-link-building-services/) says outreach is done "without using black-hat tactics like private blog networks or bots".

**Watch for:**
- The same page sells guest posts on gambling sites, so ask about anchors and labeling.
- The TwinSpires interview gives no traffic numbers, racing wagering is a different product from online casino, and we found no award-body recognition.

### 5. Toucan International: Best for Betting Affiliates

Toucan International won Best Digital Agency at the [iGB Affiliate Awards 2026](https://www.igbaffiliate.com/en/articles/people/brian-christopher-and-blask-stand-out-in-igb-affiliate-awards-2026/), ahead of a [shortlist](https://www.igbaffiliate.com/en/articles/people/igb-affiliate-awards-2026-shortlist-revealed/) that included TAG Media and SEOBrothers. Its named clients are mostly sports betting affiliates: Pickswise, Checkd Group and Footy Accumulators. The one client we could check on a register, EveryMatrix, is a B2B gaming supplier licensed by the UK Gambling Commission.

The [case studies on its homepage](https://toucan.international/) are specific about outcomes. One claims position one for key NFL terms for Pickswise, with 12-month link targets hit in month four. Another reports 67% more first-time deposits for Checkd Group's betting brands, and EveryMatrix is listed with a 70% rise in non-branded organic clicks.

**Watch for:**
- Toucan was [formed in 2025 from Checkd Group's digital PR and SEO division](https://g3newswire.com/toucan-international-year-one-growth-hits-230/), so ask which Checkd results predate the split.
- Every bookmaker we sampled on Footy Accumulators held a UK license. Pickswise, though, also promotes ProphetX and Novig, prediction-market books that [Connecticut](https://portal.ct.gov/governor/news/press-releases/2026/09-2026/governor-lamont-announces-new-actions-against-illegal-prediction-markets-offering-sports-wagers) and [Ohio](https://covers.com/industry/ohio-regulator-sends-cease-and-desist-notices-to-10-prediction-market-companies-oct-5-2026) regulators have told to stop offering sports bets. Its pages do not say whether Toucan worked on those listings.
- It takes six clients at a time, and none of the pages we read say how links are sourced.

### 6. Cedarwood Digital: Best for UK Affiliates

Cedarwood Digital won Best Use of Search, Gaming (Small) at the European Search Awards two years running: in 2025 with Slotozilla, and in [2026 with Best New Bingo Sites](https://europeansearchawards.com/2026-winners/). It is a Manchester digital marketing agency rather than a gambling specialist, and both wins were for off-site SEO and digital PR.

Its [link building page](https://cedarwood.digital/link-building-agency/) says "we don't buy links or work with link networks", the clearest stance among the six. Every bingo operator we checked on Best New Bingo Sites held a UK license.

**Watch for:**
- It has no dedicated gambling service page, and its gambling clients are affiliates.
- Archived copies of Slotozilla's UK section from October 2026 listed casinos we could not find on the UK Gambling Commission register. Ask which markets and operators the agency's work would promote.

The per-link model carries the most risk for a licensed brand. A placement bought to pass ranking value breaks Google's link spam rules unless it carries `rel="sponsored"` or `rel="nofollow"`, and the agency, not you, controls the list of sites.

### Is a Specialist Worth It?

A specialist usually earns its higher fee when you need links and content in regulated markets. In the forum threads we read, some general SEOs said they avoid gambling clients, and a specialist already knows each market's advertising rules. If your in-house team handles technical SEO and content, you may only need a specialist for digital PR.

Organic search also carries more of the load in this vertical, because Google only allows online gambling ads in [approved countries, from certified advertisers](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/15132179?hl=en).

## How to Vet an iGaming SEO Agency in 20 Minutes

A bad hire costs more than the fee. One affiliate on the GPWA forum wrote that an SEO company that "came well recommended" [caused them to lose 89% of their organic traffic](https://www.gpwa.org/forum/why-igaming-search-engine-optimization-such-challenge-right-now-270556.html). These six checks, run before you sign, would catch most of the problems we saw. The first three take about 20 minutes; the rest depend on the agency's replies.

</figure>

1. **Look up every client on an official list.** Use the UK Gambling Commission register, the Malta Gaming Authority's licensee register for many EU brands, iGaming Ontario's operator list, or a state list such as New Jersey's Division of Gaming Enforcement. A client you cannot find, or one licensed only offshore, tells you what market the agency really works in.
2. **Open one case study and look for four things:** the client's name, the market, the dates and the starting point. "Traffic up 300%" with none of those reads like a brochure. Then ask the agency to put you in touch with that client before you sign, and to name who will work on your account.
3. **Check awards on the organizer's own page.** The EGR, European Search Awards and iGB pages list winners by category. A badge that does not appear there does not count.
4. **Ask how links are bought and labeled.** If the answer includes "per link", "niche edits" or "guest posts", ask whether those links carry `rel="sponsored"`. Ask to see the list of sites before anything goes live. Ask how they would handle a disavow, and avoid any plan built on expired domains bought and repurposed mainly to rank low-value pages, which Google's spam policies name as abuse. Our comparison of [link building platforms](https://geotoolbox.ai/blog/best-link-building-platforms) explains how paid placements are sold.
5. **Ask about risky tactics and conflicts.** An agency placing casino pages on unrelated sites mainly to borrow their ranking signals, which Google's spam policies handle under a site reputation policy, one mass-producing pages mainly to manipulate rankings, which they call scaled content abuse, or one working for unlicensed brands is betting with your domain. Also ask whether it runs its own casino affiliate sites in your market, since those would compete with you, and who checks each page against each market's advertising rules, from license details to [safer gambling messages](https://www.asa.org.uk/type/non_broadcast/code_section/16.html) and affiliate disclosures.
6. **Read the draft contract, not just the proposal.** Look for who owns the links and content if you leave, the notice period, a right to end the deal over a compliance breach, and reporting tied to registrations and first-time deposits rather than rankings alone. In the UK, [license condition 1.1.2](https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/licensees-and-businesses/lccp/condition/1-1-2-responsibility-for-third-parties-all-licences) makes operators responsible for the third parties they contract with and sets terms those contracts must include. Receptional's LiveScore Bet case, for example, reports lifetime value generated from casino pages, a better measure than traffic.

Alongside these checks, a technical [SEO audit](https://geotoolbox.ai/blog/seo-audit) of your own site gives you a baseline to judge the agency's first three months against.

### How Long Results Take

How fast results come depends on the keywords. The LiveScore Bet result above came from an established betting brand. For a new site chasing money keywords, an affiliate on the GPWA forum put it at ["2 years or more if you even make it at all"](https://www.gpwa.org/forum/search-engine-optimization-results-268847.html). Agree on the baseline and the review date in writing.
