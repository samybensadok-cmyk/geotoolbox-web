## How We Check Each Deal

We open each vendor's own deal and pricing page in a logged-out browser on a US connection, copy the offer and the price shown, and save the page with its capture time. Nothing comes from a coupon site, and a vendor's old offer is labelled as old.

Each deal gets one of seven statuses:

- **Live:** the vendor's page shows the offer today.
- **Standing:** a labelled discount with no end date that has sat on the page for months, so we do not count it as a sale.
- **Announced:** the terms are published and start later.
- **Unconfirmed:** the page shows last year's offer with no year, so we do not call it live.
- **Teaser:** the page promises a sale but gives no terms.
- **No deal yet:** the vendor's pages show nothing for 2026.
- **Expired:** the offer ended, and the row stays struck through until December 2.

Commissions never change a status. If a vendor pays us and has nothing live, the table says so. The same goes for tools whose pages show a past or ended offer, such as Surfer and NeuronWriter: that is "No deal yet."

## How to Judge a Black Friday SEO Deal

A deal is only good if you would buy the tool at the renewal price. Check these in order before you pay:

1. **The renewal price.** SE Ranking, Rank Math and WP Rocket discount the first term only, and Kinsta's discount covers the first 3 months. Write down the first year price and the price it renews at, and decide whether you still want the tool at that number.
2. **The billing term.** Snov.io's terms exclude monthly plans, and Kinsta's monthly offer ends after 3 months.
3. **Code or automatic.** SE Ranking, WP Rocket and Rank Math apply the price automatically and Kinsta shows no code, while Snov.io and Link Whisper need a coupon code at checkout.
4. **The limits.** Sites, seats, prompts and credits decide whether the plan fits. WP Rocket's Single license covers 1 site, Plus covers 3, and Link Whisper's bonus credits expire after 90 days.
5. **The way out.** Kinsta lists a 30-day money-back guarantee and WP Rocket a 14-day free trial. Snov.io says Black Friday purchases are non-refundable, and for the others you should confirm the refund terms still apply to the discounted plan.
6. **The weak spot.** Decide whether your SEO is held back by keywords, technical fixes or authority, and buy the tool that covers that. Buying a suite you will barely use, or two overlapping plugins or writing tools, only adds cost.
7. **The 12-month return.** Add up what the tool costs over the next year at the renewal price and ask what it will earn or save you.

"Lifetime" needs its own check. On the Mangools, Scalenut and Frase pages we archived from 2023 to 2025, it meant a discount that lasts until you cancel, not a one-time payment. Marketplace "lifetime deals" are a different thing, so read which one you are being offered.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When do SEO tool Black Friday sales start?

It depends on the vendor. Kinsta started on November 9, WP Rocket's page was live by November 5 and SE Ranking launched on November 17, while Snov.io and Link Whisper start on November 23 and 24. Surfer and NeuronWriter have waited for Black Friday week in past years.

### Does Ahrefs or Semrush have a Black Friday deal?

Not today. Ahrefs says on its help page that it runs no seasonal specials, and Semrush's pricing page shows only its standing annual saving. We update the table if either changes.

### Are first-year Black Friday discounts worth it?

Yes, if you would keep the tool at the renewal price, because the saving applies once. SE Ranking, Rank Math and WP Rocket discount the first term only, and Kinsta's discount covers the first 3 months.

### Is a lifetime deal the same as a lifetime discount?

No. A lifetime discount keeps the lower price for as long as you stay subscribed, which is how Mangools, Scalenut and Frase used the word on the pages we archived. A lifetime deal on a marketplace is usually a one-time payment, so read the terms.
