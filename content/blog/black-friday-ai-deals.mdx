**ChatGPT.** [OpenAI's pricing page](https://chatgpt.com/pricing/) lists Free at $0, Go at $8 a month (it may include ads), Plus at $20 and Pro from $100. **Claude.** [Claude's pricing page](https://claude.com/pricing) shows Pro at $20 a month, or $17 a month on annual billing ($200 upfront), with Max from $100 and no promo banner. See our [Claude pricing guide](https://geotoolbox.ai/blog/claude-pricing) for the tiers.

**Google.** [Google's AI plans page](https://one.google.com/about/google-ai-plans/) lists AI Plus at $4.99 a month, AI Pro at $19.99 (or $199.99 on annual billing) and Ultra from $99.99. Students get AI Pro free for a year, which the page lists as a student offer rather than a Black Friday deal. On November 24 a banner gave members early access to up to 50% off select plans for Black Friday; on November 27 it was gone, and the page showed no Black Friday price. Our [Gemini pricing guide](https://geotoolbox.ai/blog/gemini-pricing) explains the tiers.

**Perplexity.** [Perplexity's Pro page](https://www.perplexity.ai/pro) shows $17 a month billed annually and a "limited time" bonus of $40 in free Computer credits. That is extra usage, not a lower price. More in our [Perplexity pricing guide](https://geotoolbox.ai/blog/perplexity-pricing).

**Grok.** On [grok.com/plans](https://grok.com/plans), a banner reads "Black Friday: Pay for 1 month, get 1 free", so Premium on monthly billing is $15 a month for the first 2 months, then $30. ## How to Tell a Real AI Deal from a Weak One

A deal on AI tools is real when the vendor's own page shows the price before, the price after and an end date. Adobe and ElevenLabs show all three. Higgsfield, Suno and Grok show a countdown instead of a date, and Scalenut shows nothing, so treat their end dates as unconfirmed.

Then check the renewal price. Adobe's Creative Cloud Pro offer shows the pattern: 12 months at the promo rate, then the regular price. That is $359.88 over the first year against $839.88 at the regular rate, and, if it renews at the regular price, the second year costs the full $839.88.

A few more checks save most of the regret:

- **Who qualifies.** Check whether the offer is for new customers only, and whether it stacks with a trial: Microsoft's page says its limited-time offer and the one-month trial cannot be combined.
- **What you are really getting.** Perplexity's $40 free Computer credits add usage and leave the price unchanged.
- **Where the code came from.** A promo code that is not printed on the vendor's own page is probably a reseller's offer.
- **Your data.** Check whether a discounted AI tool trains on your inputs and whether that is opt-in or opt-out. Anthropic's [privacy page](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/10023580-is-my-data-used-for-model-training) says Claude Free, Pro and Max chats can be used to improve its models if you choose to allow it.
- **A trial on real work.** Run a week of your actual work through the free plan or trial before paying for a year, so you meet the credit limits before the discount ends.

Opening a new account to catch a promo may conflict with a vendor's terms, so read them first, and a new account may not carry over your history.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the cheapest way to pay for an AI assistant?

First check whether a free tier at $0 is enough. Beyond that, annual billing, a student plan or the entry tier. [Claude Pro](https://claude.com/pricing) is $17 a month on annual billing instead of $20, [Google AI Pro](https://one.google.com/about/google-ai-plans/) is $199.99 a year, [ChatGPT Go](https://chatgpt.com/pricing/) costs $8 a month, and students get Google AI Pro free for a year.

### Which AI is cheaper than ChatGPT?

[Google AI Plus](https://one.google.com/about/google-ai-plans/) at $4.99 a month is the cheapest paid assistant plan we captured, against $8 for [ChatGPT Go](https://chatgpt.com/pricing/) and $20 for Plus. ChatGPT, Claude and Google also have free tiers at $0. Our [ChatGPT alternatives guide](https://geotoolbox.ai/blog/chatgpt-alternatives) sorts the options by job.

### Are AI lifetime deals worth it?

Rarely for a main assistant. Every prompt costs the vendor compute, so lifetime plans tend to cap credits, and the vendor must stay in business for years. A fair test is whether you would use the tool for 3 years and the vendor has already operated for 2. None of the pricing pages for ChatGPT, Claude, Google or Perplexity lists a lifetime plan.

### When do AI Black Friday deals start and end?

Black Friday is November 27 and Cyber Monday is November 30, 2026. The live offers above with a printed end date finish between November 27 (Adobe Creative Cloud Pro) and December 31 (GitHub Copilot Fusion), and several print no end date. We re-check the table daily until December 2.
