## How We Evaluated These Tools

We looked at each tool on the criteria buyers actually weigh, not feature-count vanity:

- **Engines covered.** ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, and Gemini are the table stakes. Coverage of all the major ones matters more than depth on one.
- **Pricing and free tier.** Whether you can start free, self-serve at a published price, or only "book a demo."
- **Scope.** Tracking (where am I cited), optimization (how do I write to get cited), or reachability (can the engine even fetch my page). These are different problems.
- **Methodology transparency.** A visibility score with no stated method is a red flag.
- **Fit.** Standalone tool, add-on to an SEO suite you already pay for, or a quick free grader.

A note on scope before the list: a tracker tells you that you are not cited, but not why. Often the why is technical, a blocked crawler or a page an engine cannot read. That gap is worth keeping in mind as you compare.

## AI Visibility Tracking Tools

## How to Choose

Match the tool to the job, not the hype:

- **You just want a baseline.** Start with a free grader (Mangools, HubSpot, or Geotoolbox). Spend nothing until you know where you stand.
- **You suspect you are invisible for technical reasons.** Check reachability first; a blocked crawler makes every other tool moot.
- **You already pay for an SEO suite.** Use its AI add-on (Semrush, SE Ranking, Ahrefs) before buying a standalone tracker.
- **You need serious, ongoing tracking across many prompts or brands.** A dedicated tracker (Profound, Peec AI, Otterly, AthenaHQ) earns its price.
- **Your problem is the content itself.** A content optimizer (Surfer, Frase) helps you write the extractable version.

## What GEO Tools Can't Do for You

A tool surfaces the gap. It does not close it. Every tracker on this list can tell you that you are under-cited; none of them rewrite your pages, earn your mentions, or fix a blocked crawler for you. That work is still yours.

Be skeptical of two claims in particular. First, any "AI visibility score" with no stated methodology, since a number you cannot interrogate is marketing, not measurement. Second, any promise of guaranteed citations, because engines retrieve and cite only a fraction of eligible pages and the set shifts constantly. The honest use of these tools is to measure direction and prioritize work, not to buy an outcome.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the best free GEO tool?**
For a quick baseline, Mangools AI Search Grader and HubSpot's AI Search Grader are fully free, and Geotoolbox is free during beta with a focus on bot reachability. Start with a free grader before paying for anything.

**Do I need a dedicated GEO tool, or is my SEO suite enough?**
If you already pay for Semrush, SE Ranking, or Ahrefs, use their AI add-on first. Add a dedicated tracker only when you need broader prompt coverage or multi-brand tracking they do not handle well.

**Which AI engines should a GEO tool track?**
At minimum ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, and Gemini. Coverage of the major engines matters more than deep features on a single one.

**How much do GEO tools cost?**
They range from free graders to roughly $29 to $99 per month for self-serve trackers, up to several hundred per month or demo-only enterprise pricing. Prices move fast, so confirm on each site.

**Are GEO tracking tools accurate?**
They sample; they do not see every AI answer. Treat the numbers as directional and prefer tools that publish their methodology over those that show a score with no explanation.

**What is the difference between a GEO tracker and a reachability checker?**
A tracker reports whether you are cited. A reachability checker confirms an AI crawler can actually fetch and read your page, which is the prerequisite for being cited at all. They solve different halves of the problem.
