Generative engine optimization (GEO) tools help you see and improve how AI engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews cite your brand. But "GEO tool" covers five very different jobs, from prompt-level visibility tracking to checking whether AI crawlers can even reach your pages. Buying the wrong category is the easiest mistake to make, so we have grouped them by use case rather than forcing a ranked list.

## How We Evaluated These Tools

We looked at each tool on the criteria buyers actually weigh, not feature-count vanity:

- **Engines covered.** ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, and Gemini are the table stakes. Coverage of all the major ones matters more than depth on one.
- **Pricing and free tier.** Whether you can start free, self-serve at a published price, or only "book a demo."
- **Scope.** Tracking (where am I cited), optimization (how do I write to get cited), or reachability (can the engine even fetch my page). These are different problems.
- **Methodology transparency.** A visibility score with no stated method is a red flag.
- **Fit.** Standalone tool, add-on to an SEO suite you already pay for, or a quick free grader.

Why this matters now: AI search is no longer a fringe channel. Pew Research found that [34% of US adults](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/06/25/34-of-us-adults-have-used-chatgpt-about-double-the-share-in-2023/) had used ChatGPT per a survey published June 2025, about double the share two years earlier. On the classic-search side, Ahrefs found that AI Overviews correlate with a [34.5% lower click-through rate](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overviews-reduce-clicks/) for the top organic result. When fewer people click and more answers come from AI summaries, being cited in those summaries is how you stay visible, which is exactly what these tools measure.

## AI Visibility Tracking Tools

These platforms answer one question: how often, and where, do AI engines mention or cite your brand. They track prompts across models and report [share of voice](https://geotoolbox.ai/glossary/share-of-voice), citations, and sentiment. This is the largest and most crowded category, and most are priced for teams rather than solo users.

### Profound

<a href="https://www.tryprofound.com" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Profound</a> is the enterprise option, with deep analytics on AI visibility, brand citations, and competitor comparisons across the major conversational models, including ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, Gemini, and Microsoft Copilot. Beyond prompt-level tracking, it surfaces which source pages the engines pull from and lets you run a high volume of custom prompts, which is where the enterprise pricing goes. It is built for larger teams; third-party reviews report entry tiers from about $99 per month (Starter) and $399 per month (Growth), with enterprise pricing custom and signup still largely demo-routed. It offers a free answer-engine report to start, so you can see a sample of your citation footprint before committing. If the enterprise pricing rules it out for you, we break down [what Profound actually costs](https://geotoolbox.ai/blog/profound-pricing) and compare the [Profound alternatives](https://geotoolbox.ai/blog/profound-alternatives) that cover the same job at a lower tier.

### Peec AI

<a href="https://peec.ai" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Peec AI</a> leans into competitive benchmarking, tracking how your brand performs against named rivals across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and Gemini, and organizing the source content cited in responses so you can see which competitor pages keep getting pulled. The prompt allowance and tracked-competitor count scale with the plan, which is the main lever on price. It starts with a free trial, with self-serve plans reported from around €89 per month and enterprise via sales. A solid pick if your priority is seeing where rivals out-cite you rather than just your own standing.

### Otterly.ai

<a href="https://otterly.ai" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Otterly.ai</a> is one of the more affordable standalone trackers, with self-serve plans from around $29 per month and a 14-day trial. All plans cover ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, and Microsoft Copilot; Gemini and Google AI Mode are paid add-ons, and the prompt allowance (15 on the entry plan) is what scales by tier. A reasonable starting point for a small team that wants prompt tracking without an enterprise contract, as long as you check the per-tier prompt cap against the keywords you actually care about.

### AthenaHQ

<a href="https://athenahq.ai" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">AthenaHQ</a> is self-serve from roughly $295 per month, or about $95 per month billed annually, and offers a free 10-minute audit so you can see your standing before paying. It tracks the major conversational engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Gemini, and more) and reports share of voice, citations, and the sources cited in responses. Good fit if you want to skip the sales call and start the same day.

### Scrunch AI

<a href="https://www.scrunch.com" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Scrunch AI</a> targets enterprise and multi-brand tracking across the major conversational engines, with workspace-style separation so an agency or a multi-product company can monitor several brands side by side. It is available via a 7-day trial, with published plans from $250 per month billed annually ($300 month-to-month) and prompt volume and the number of tracked brands set at the plan level. Worth a look if you manage visibility across several brands and need them reported separately rather than rolled into one account.

### Writesonic

<a href="https://writesonic.com" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Writesonic</a> combines AI-search tracking with AI writing, so the same tool that flags a gap can help you draft the fix. The tracking side reports share of voice and citations, but engine coverage is tiered: the entry plan tracks ChatGPT only, Gemini and Google AI Overviews arrive around $199 per month, and the full engine set is enterprise-only. The writing side draws on the same prompt data to brief new content. Plans start around $99 per month after a 7-day trial, and the prompt allowance and word or generation limits scale with the tier. Best if you want tracking and content generation under one roof rather than paying for two tools, though neither half is as deep as a dedicated specialist.

## How to Choose

Match the tool to the job, not the hype. If you think of this as answer engine optimization, the same split holds, and we break down the [best AEO tools by job](https://geotoolbox.ai/blog/best-aeo-tools) the same way. Work down this list in order and stop at the first one that fits:

1. **You just want a baseline.** Start with a free grader (Mangools, HubSpot, or Geotoolbox). Spend nothing until you know where you stand.
2. **You suspect you are invisible for technical reasons.** Check reachability first; a blocked crawler makes every other tool moot.
3. **You already pay for an SEO suite.** Use its AI add-on (Semrush, SE Ranking, Ahrefs) before buying a standalone tracker.
4. **You need serious, ongoing tracking across many prompts or brands.** A dedicated tracker (Profound, Peec AI, Otterly, AthenaHQ) earns its price.
5. **Your problem is the content itself.** A content optimizer (Surfer, Frase) helps you write the extractable version.

## What GEO Tools Can't Do for You

A tool surfaces the gap. It does not close it. These tools can tell you that you are under-cited; none of them rewrite your pages or earn your mentions for you. That work is still yours, which is the real choice behind [GEO services vs software](https://geotoolbox.ai/blog/geo-services-vs-software): do it in-house or hire it out.

Be skeptical of two claims in particular. First, any [AI visibility score](https://geotoolbox.ai/blog/ai-visibility-score) with no stated methodology, since a number you cannot interrogate is marketing, not measurement. Second, any promise of guaranteed citations, because engines retrieve and cite only a fraction of eligible pages and the set shifts constantly. The honest use of these tools is to measure direction and prioritize work, not to buy an outcome.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the best free GEO tool?**
For a quick baseline, Mangools AI Search Grader and HubSpot's AI Search Grader are fully free, and Geotoolbox's free tier covers bot reachability plus a starter visibility scan. Start with a free grader before paying for anything.

**Do I need a dedicated GEO tool, or is my SEO suite enough?**
Try your suite's AI add-on first; bundled with a subscription you already pay for, it usually costs less than a standalone tracker. A dedicated tracker earns its price when you need broader prompt coverage, more engines, or multi-brand tracking the add-on does not handle well.

**Which AI engines should a GEO tool track?**
At minimum ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, and Gemini. Match the list against where your audience actually asks questions; a tool that goes deep on one engine your buyers never use is wasted coverage.

**How much do GEO tools cost?**
They range from free graders to roughly $29 to $99 per month for self-serve trackers, up to several hundred per month or demo-only enterprise pricing. Prices move fast, so confirm on each site.

**Are GEO tracking tools accurate?**
They sample; they do not see every AI answer. Treat the numbers as directional and prefer tools that publish their methodology over those that show a score with no explanation.

**What is the difference between a GEO tracker and a reachability checker?**
A tracker reports whether you are cited. A reachability checker confirms an AI crawler can actually fetch and read your page, which is the prerequisite for being cited at all. They solve different halves of the problem.
