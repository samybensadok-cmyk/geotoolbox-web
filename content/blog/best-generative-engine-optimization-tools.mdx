## How We Evaluated These Tools

We looked at each tool on the criteria buyers actually weigh, not feature-count vanity:

- **Engines covered.** ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, and Gemini are the table stakes. Coverage of all the major ones matters more than depth on one.
- **Pricing and free tier.** Whether you can start free, self-serve at a published price, or only "book a demo."
- **Scope.** Tracking (where am I cited), optimization (how do I write to get cited), or reachability (can the engine even fetch my page). These are different problems.
- **Methodology transparency.** A visibility score with no stated method is a red flag.
- **Fit.** Standalone tool, add-on to an SEO suite you already pay for, or a quick free grader.

## AI Visibility Tracking Tools

These trackers, often sold as AI visibility platforms, answer one question: how often, and where, do AI engines mention or cite your brand. They track prompts across models and report [share of voice](https://geotoolbox.ai/glossary/share-of-voice), citations, and sentiment. This is the largest and most crowded category, and most are priced for teams rather than solo users.

### Profound

<a href="https://www.tryprofound.com" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Profound</a> is the enterprise option, with deep analytics on AI visibility, brand citations, and competitor comparisons. Engine coverage is tier-gated, though: the $99 Starter tracks ChatGPT only (50 prompts), the $399 Growth adds Perplexity and Google AI Overviews, and the full set of up to 9 answer engines, including Gemini, Copilot, Grok, DeepSeek, and Claude, is enterprise-only. Beyond prompt-level tracking, it surfaces which source pages the engines pull from and lets you run a high volume of custom prompts, which is where the enterprise pricing goes. It is built for larger teams, and the backing matches: Profound <a href="https://www.tryprofound.com/blog/profound-raises-96m-series-c" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">raised a $96 million Series C at a $1 billion valuation</a> in February 2026, the largest war chest in this category. Enterprise tiers stay custom as of July 2026, and third-party reviews still cite a $499 Lite tier the vendor page no longer shows. It offers a free AEO Report to start, so you can see a sample of your citation footprint before committing. If the enterprise pricing rules it out for you, we break down [what Profound actually costs](https://geotoolbox.ai/blog/profound-pricing) and compare the [Profound alternatives](https://geotoolbox.ai/blog/profound-alternatives) that cover the same job at a lower tier.

## How to Choose

Match the tool to the job, not the hype. If you think of this as answer engine optimization, the same split holds, and we break down the [best AEO tools by job](https://geotoolbox.ai/blog/best-aeo-tools) the same way. Work down this list in order and stop at the first one that fits:

1. **You just want a baseline.** Start with a free grader (Mangools, HubSpot, or Geotoolbox). Spend nothing until you know where you stand.
2. **You suspect you are invisible for technical reasons.** Check reachability first; a blocked crawler makes every other tool moot.
3. **You already pay for an SEO suite.** Use its AI add-on (Semrush, SE Ranking, Ahrefs) before buying a standalone tracker.
4. **You need serious, ongoing tracking across many prompts or brands.** A dedicated tracker (Profound, Peec AI, Otterly, Rankscale, AthenaHQ, Scrunch, or our own Geotoolbox if you want the reachability layer on the same bill) earns its price.
5. **Your problem is the content itself.** A [content optimizer](https://geotoolbox.ai/blog/best-content-optimization-tools) (Surfer, Frase) helps you write the extractable version.

## What GEO Tools Can't Do for You

A tool surfaces the gap. It does not close it. These tools can tell you that you are under-cited; none of them rewrite your pages or earn your mentions for you. That work is still yours, which is the real choice behind [GEO services vs software](https://geotoolbox.ai/blog/geo-services-vs-software): do it in-house or hire it out.

Be skeptical of two claims in particular. First, any [AI visibility score](https://geotoolbox.ai/blog/ai-visibility-score) with no stated methodology, since a number you cannot interrogate is marketing, not measurement. Second, any promise of guaranteed citations, because engines retrieve and cite only a fraction of eligible pages and the set shifts constantly. A [critical survey of 45 GEO studies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14035) published on arXiv in July 2026 reached the same conclusion: no optimization technique reviewed showed stable effects across all AI platforms. The honest use of these tools is to measure direction and prioritize work, not to buy an outcome.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best free GEO tool?

For a quick baseline, Mangools AI Search Grader and HubSpot's AEO Grader are fully free, and Geotoolbox's free tools cover the robots.txt half of reachability, and its paid tracking opens with a 7-day free trial. Start with a free grader before paying for anything.

### Do I need a dedicated GEO tool, or is my SEO suite enough?

Try your suite's AI add-on first; bundled with a subscription you already pay for, it usually costs less than a standalone tracker. A dedicated tracker earns its price when you need broader prompt coverage, more engines, or multi-brand tracking the add-on does not handle well.

### Which AI engines should a GEO tool track?

At minimum ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, and Gemini. Match the list against where your audience actually asks questions; a tool that goes deep on one engine your buyers never use is wasted coverage.

### How much do GEO tools cost?

They range from free graders to roughly $20 to $99 per month for self-serve trackers, up to several hundred per month or demo-only enterprise pricing. Prices move fast, so confirm on each site.

### Are GEO tracking tools accurate?

They sample; they do not see every AI answer. Treat the numbers as directional and prefer tools that publish their methodology over those that show a score with no explanation.

### Do GEO tools track brand mentions without links?

Most trackers do: they count every time an engine names your brand in an answer, whether or not it links or cites your site. The better ones report mentions and citations separately, which matters because an unlinked mention builds awareness but sends no traffic.

### Why do my AI citation numbers swing from month to month?

Because engines generate answers dynamically and trackers sample them, some volatility is baked in: the same prompt can cite you one week and not the next. Treat a single month's swing as noise and look for sustained direction over two or three months before changing strategy.

### What is the difference between a GEO tracker and a reachability checker?

A tracker reports whether you are cited. A reachability checker confirms an AI crawler can actually fetch and read your page, which is the prerequisite for being cited at all. They solve different halves of the problem.
