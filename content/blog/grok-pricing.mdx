Grok is free to start, and for a lot of people that is the end of the story. If you want more, new sign-ups now buy **The X Subscription**, which xAI launched on October 12, 2026 and is rolling out globally: Plus at $8 a month, Premium at $30, Super at $100, and Ultra at $200. Every paid tier bundles Grok with Cursor's coding agents and X Premium perks. xAI says the old SuperGrok plans are closed to new sign-ups, though existing subscribers keep them. Metered developer API use is billed per token, from $1 per million input tokens.

That change makes many Grok pricing guides out of date, because most were written around SuperGrok. Below are the current Grok plan prices and text-model API rates, taken from xAI's own pricing pages and docs, plus the question the numbers exist to answer: which plan, if any, you should pay for. A quick disambiguation first, because the search results mix them: this is about xAI's Grok chatbot, not the unrelated "Grok" crypto token.

## Is Grok Free? What the Free Tier Actually Gives You

Yes. Grok has a free tier, and it is now the Free plan of The X Subscription. Per [xAI's announcement](https://x.ai/news/x-subscription), it gives you daily access to Grok Chat, Imagine, Voice, and Build, a starter allowance for Cursor's coding agents, and standard X. You can sign in at grok.com and start chatting, with real-time answers pulled from the web and X.

The catch is the cap. xAI lists Free usage only as "limited" and does not publish a number, so try Free with your own workload before assuming it will cover you. Lean on Grok for real work and you will hit it fast, which is the signal to consider paying.

Media is where the free tier has moved the most. The [Grok Imagine](https://geotoolbox.ai/blog/grok-imagine) creator was effectively free for casual users at launch, until users reported around March 20 that it had been [effectively locked to paying subscribers](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/rkbynj99bx). xAI made no official statement; replies from the @grok account called the change temporary. The new Free plan lists limited Imagine access again, but Premium is the first tier that lists image and video creation as a headline feature. Treat the free plan as a useful way to try Grok and handle light tasks, not as a substitute for a paid plan if you generate media or run long sessions.

## The X Subscription Tiers: Plus vs Premium vs Super vs Ultra

The paid tiers are one product in different sizes. Each step up mostly buys a bigger share of the weekly usage pool, with a few features gated to Premium and above. Prices below are from [xAI's pricing page](https://x.ai/pricing) and its docs.

### Plus ($8 a Month)

The entry tier. It gives you a base usage pool shared across Grok Chat, Voice, Imagine and Build, the Grok API and Cursor, plus an X checkmark, creator tools, fewer timeline ads and advanced analytics on X. What it leaves out matters: **Plus does not include Grok Bot**, xAI's agent product, and it is not available on team plans. It fits someone who mostly chats with Grok or posts on X and wants more headroom than Free.

### Premium ($30 a Month)

The plan xAI labels "most popular", and the one most paying users will want. It carries 4x the Plus usage, Grok Bot, the latest Grok models, faster chat responses, image and video creation, and Cursor's cloud agents. On the X side you get the full X Premium+ benefits, including ad-free scrolling on most surfaces. xAI describes it as "the benefits of SuperGrok, Cursor Pro, and X Premium+, previously $90/month combined". On price and listed features it is the nearest equivalent to the old $30 SuperGrok, though xAI publishes no usage numbers to confirm the allowance matches.

### Super ($100 a Month)

Everything in Premium with 15x the Plus usage, priority model access at peak times, and what xAI lists as faster responses and generations. It suits heavy daily users of Grok Bot or Cursor agents.

### Ultra ($200 a Month)

The top individual tier: 50x the Plus usage, what xAI lists as the fastest responses, and the earliest access to new products and models. Most people will never need that much usage.

<Callout type="note">
Different activities draw different amounts from the weekly pool, so the allowance is hard to predict in advance. Before you commit to a tier, use it for a week and watch the remaining usage and reset date in your account settings. If you run out mid-week, you can buy top-ups (usage credits) instead of upgrading, or wait for the weekly refresh.
</Callout>

## What Happened to SuperGrok and X Premium?

Until October 12, 2026, Grok was sold through overlapping routes: the standalone SuperGrok line at grok.com (SuperGrok Lite at $10, SuperGrok at $30, SuperGrok Plus at $100 and SuperGrok Heavy at $300 a month; Heavy ran a $99 promo for its first 3 months in June), and the X Premium and Premium+ bundles for the social platform at about [$8 and $40](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/x-premium). It was easy to buy the wrong one.

The X Subscription replaced both for new customers. In [xAI's words](https://x.ai/news/x-subscription), "the previous plans are no longer available for new sign-ups." One lag to note: when geotoolbox checked on October 13, the US App Store listing for the Grok app still showed the SuperGrok tiers as in-app purchases. That is store metadata, not a sign-up route, so trust the plan screen inside the app. If you already pay for one of them, here is what that means in practice, per xAI's [switching docs](https://docs.x.ai/x-subscription/overview):

- **You do not have to switch.** Existing SuperGrok, X Premium and Cursor plans continue at their current price and features unless you change or cancel them.
- **Switching is permanent.** When you move, the old subscriptions you select are cancelled with a prorated refund for unused time, and xAI support cannot undo it.
- **Switching comes with offers.** xAI shows limited-time switch offers in your billing settings. Your new plan is charged at full price, and the refund for the old one arrives separately.
- **Cursor users lose included third-party usage.** On a current Cursor plan, included Claude or GPT usage continues until you switch. On the X Subscription, third-party models in Cursor are available only on Premium and above and bill at API list prices from the first use.
- **X Premium+ benefits need Premium or higher.** Plus includes only the lighter X perks (checkmark, creator tools, fewer ads), so a Premium+ subscriber who switches to Plus loses features.
- **App store purchases follow store rules.** Grok, Grok Bot or X Premium bought through the App Store are cancelled by xAI when you switch, but Apple decides the refund. A Cursor plan billed through the App Store is not cancelled for you: cancel it in your iPhone or iPad Settings. For Google Play purchases, check afterwards that the old subscription is not still set to renew.

xAI has said it will eventually move remaining customers to the new plans, with no timeline yet and advance notice promised. Before you switch, compare your current plan's limits with the tier you would land on, since xAI only states that, across standard-priced plans, each comparable new tier gives more usage per dollar.

Note that xAI's pricing page now lists "Grok API" among the Plus features and says the weekly pool covers API access, while its subscription docs mention only Grok and Cursor surfaces. The current flagship is **grok-4.7**, [released September 21, 2026](https://x.ai/news/grok-4-7), at the same $2 input and $6 output per million tokens and 500K-token context window as [grok-4.6](https://geotoolbox.ai/blog/grok-4-6) (August 12, 2026) before it. Watch the context tier, because the headline price is only half the story: xAI's docs list grok-4.7 at $2 input / $6 output for prompts **under 200K tokens**, and **$4 / $12 from 200K tokens**, with cached input at $0.50 (rising to $1 from 200K). Since the model's selling point is a 500K window, any prompt that uses it bills at the higher rate, and that rate applies to every token in the request, not just the part past 200K. Every xAI text model in the table doubles the same way. xAI also serves a Grok 4.7 Fast variant at twice the standard token rates (1.5x for long-context requests), but only through Cursor and Grok Build, not as a public API model.

grok-4.7 is pricier than the older grok-4.3 ($1.25/$2.50), but still under [Claude Opus 5.5](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/pricing) ($4/$20) and well under Claude Fable 5.1 ($10/$50), Anthropic's top generally available model. OpenAI's GPT-6 Sol and its newer [GPT-6.1 Sol](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/pricing) both list at $2 input and $10 output, so they match grok-4.7 on input and cost more on output. Google's [Gemini 4 Argon](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing), generally available since October 13, 2026, sits at the same $2/$10 on an introductory price through November 30, then $4/$20.

The official docs list a tighter set of models than the third-party pricing aggregators do. <Callout type="note">
A quirk worth knowing: on the consumer apps you often cannot tell which Grok model answered a given question, because tiers route between models automatically and the interface does not show it. The API is the opposite. You choose an explicit, versioned model ID, so the API is the closest you get to knowing what you are running, with one caveat: retired IDs are redirected to newer models.
</Callout>

## How to Use Grok (and Where to Access It)

You can reach Grok in several places. One X Subscription covers the apps, X and Cursor, and xAI says its pool includes some API access; metered API use is billed per token.

1. **grok.com (web).** The simplest path. Go to grok.com, sign in, and start chatting on the free tier.
2. **The X app.** Grok is built into X as the Grok tab, and it draws on the same usage pool as your X Subscription plan.
3. **The standalone Grok app.** xAI publishes a dedicated Grok app for iOS and Android, which uses the same account and subscription as grok.com.
4. **Cursor.** Grok models now run inside Cursor's coding agents on the same pool. Third-party models in Cursor, such as Claude or GPT, are available on Premium and above and billed separately at API list prices.
5. **The xAI API.** For developers. You create a key in the xAI developer console and call the models directly, billed per token as covered above.

For most people, the answer is grok.com on the web or the standalone app, on the free tier to start. Upgrade only once you have hit the free limits enough to know you need more. Prices differ by region (grok.com shows euro prices to European visitors). And [xAI says](https://x.ai/pricing) subscriptions are sold only directly by xAI (its site and official apps), with no authorized resellers: accounts bought from a third party "may be suspended or terminated at any time", so skip the discounted resale offers.

## Canceling, Refunds, and Free Trials

Cancellation trips people up because it depends on where you paid. X Subscription plans bought on the web are managed from your account dashboard at grok.com, where you can change plans or cancel. If you subscribed through the Apple App Store or Google Play, you cancel in that store's subscription list, because the store is what charges you. The quickest way to find out which one applies is to check your card statement or the store you used to sign up.

Start with the basics: xAI's pricing page says you can cancel anytime; to avoid the next charge, do it at least 24 hours before renewal. You keep your benefits through the end of the billing period. Canceling stops the next charge rather than refunding the current one. The exception is switching from an old SuperGrok or X Premium plan billed directly by xAI, which triggers an automatic prorated refund for the unused time; for App Store purchases, Apple decides the refund.

The free trial has also changed. The old SuperGrok plan often came with a seven-day free trial offer; xAI's US pricing page for the X Subscription lists no trial. When geotoolbox checked grok.com from Europe on October 13, Premium, Super and Ultra showed 50% off the first month instead. Offers like this vary by region and account, so read the checkout screen before you confirm.

## Which Grok Plan Should You Actually Pay For?

Most people overbuy. Here is how to decide, from the bottom up.

**Stay free** if you ask Grok a handful of questions a day and do not generate much media. The free tier may cover that, and you will know you have outgrown it the day you keep hitting the limit mid-task.

**Pay $8 for Plus** if you use Grok regularly but want the lowest paid price, or you mainly want the X checkmark and creator tools. Skip it if you want Grok Bot.

**Pay $30 for Premium** if Grok is one of your main AI tools. It is the plan xAI recommends for new AI users and the entry tier for Grok Bot, and it makes most sense if you also use Grok models in Cursor or want X Premium+.

**Pay $100 for Super** if Premium's weekly pool keeps running out before the reset.

**Pay $200 for Ultra** only if you run heavy agent, coding or video workloads every day. For everyone else it is more than they will use.

**Keep your old plan** if you already pay for SuperGrok or X Premium and it covers your needs. Nothing forces you to move yet, and switching cannot be undone.

**Use the API instead of a plan** if you are building software. A subscription buys one person a usage pool; the API meters whatever your code does, and grok-4.3 is usually the right model for programmatic work.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does Grok cost per month?

Grok is free to start. Since October 12, 2026, new sign-ups choose The X Subscription: Plus at $8 a month, Premium at $30, Super at $100, and Ultra at $200, with yearly billing at $80, $300, $1,000 and $2,000. Team seats cost $30, $100 or $200, and metered developer API use is billed per token (xAI also lists API access in the subscription pool without saying how much).

### Is SuperGrok still available?

Not for new customers, according to xAI. SuperGrok Lite, SuperGrok, SuperGrok Plus and SuperGrok Heavy closed to new sign-ups on October 12, 2026, when xAI launched The X Subscription, although the US App Store listing still showed them as in-app purchases on October 13. If you already pay for SuperGrok, your plan continues at the same price unless you switch or cancel, and switching is permanent. The $30 Premium tier includes the benefits of SuperGrok, Cursor Pro and X Premium+.

### Is the Grok free tier enough?

For light use, yes. The Free plan gives you daily, limited access to Grok Chat, Imagine, Voice, and Build, with real-time answers from the web and X, plus a starter allowance for Cursor. xAI does not publish the free limit, so you will hit the wall quickly on long sessions or heavy media use.

### Is Grok cheaper than ChatGPT?

It depends on the tier. Plus at $8 costs the same as ChatGPT Go and less than ChatGPT Plus at $20, but it leaves out Grok Bot; Premium at $30 costs more than ChatGPT Plus while bundling Cursor and X Premium+ benefits. On the API, grok-4.3 at $1.25 input and $2.50 output per million tokens is cheaper than [GPT-6.1 Sol](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/pricing) at $2 and $10, and grok-4.7 matches GPT-6.1 Sol on input while costing less on output.
