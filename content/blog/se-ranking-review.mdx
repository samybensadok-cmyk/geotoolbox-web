SE Ranking built its name as the cheaper all-in-one SEO tool with a rank tracker you could trust. This SE Ranking review asks whether that still holds at the end of 2026, after a plan overhaul, repeated rank-tracking incidents and a cut to its AI tracking. One disclosure first: we sell an AI visibility tracker that competes with SE Ranking's SE Visible.

## SE Ranking Review: The Short Answer

**SE Ranking is still a capable, easy SEO suite with good value per tracked keyword, but 2026 exposed its weak spots. Verdict: 3.3/5.**

Daily rank tracking by location and device, solid audits and quick client reports are all still there. What changed: the cheapest plan roughly doubled in price in February, a Google change disrupted rank trackers across the industry in September and left SE Ranking users with weeks of unreliable positions, and the AI prompts included in each plan were cut to 25 a day.

Our weighted score is 3.3 out of 5, and the lowest category, rank tracking reliability, is where 2026 hurt users most.

Our score sits below those averages because it weighs what changed in 2026, not years of accumulated reviews. Recent reviews are still mostly positive: Trustpilot's own summary says "reviewers had a great experience with this company". Trustpilot also notes that SE Ranking "hasn't invited customers recently, so reviews may not be representative" (the same page lists Semrush at 1.6 and Ahrefs at 2.1), and several G2 reviews on its first page are labelled "Source: Seller invite".

## What SE Ranking Still Does Well

Ease of use is the most common strength in G2's summary of SE Ranking reviews (tagged in 47 of them), ahead of features (34), keyword research (33) and rank tracking (32).

### Tracking Volume for the Money

The Core plan includes 2k keywords tracked daily across 10 projects for $129 a month. [Semrush's entry SEO plan](https://www.semrush.com/pricing/seo-ai-search/) costs $139.95 a month for 500 daily keywords and 5 websites. Each keyword counts once per search engine you track, but if you follow many keywords across many small sites, SE Ranking still gives you more room per dollar.

### Reports Clients Can Read

The plans connect Google Analytics, Search Console, Matomo and Looker Studio (listed as Data Studio), so rankings and traffic can sit in one client report. With the Agency Pack add-on they become white-label reports under your own brand. Our [white-label SEO reporting comparison](https://geotoolbox.ai/blog/white-label-seo-reports) covers nine dedicated reporting tools.

### One Plan, Most Jobs

Keyword research, competitor research, backlinks, site audits, on-page checks, local marketing and content tools all sit in the base plan. Core also includes 25K API credits and MCP access, so you can pull data into scripts or an AI assistant.

### Granular Tracking Settings

[SE Ranking's rank tracker](https://seranking.com/position-tracking.html) checks Google, Bing, Yahoo and YouTube daily, by device and location. That suits local clients who care about one city rather than one country.

## Rank Tracking Reliability in 2026

Rank tracking had two rough patches in 2026, and SE Ranking acknowledged both. The second began with a Google change that disrupted rank trackers across the industry, and SE Ranking users reported missing or wrong positions for weeks before a fix.

In February, SE Ranking replied to Trustpilot complaints that "The inaccuracies were caused by a major Google update that disrupted SEO providers industry-wide", and that the issue had since been resolved. One reviewer wrote that after a mid-March Google update, ranking tracking "no longer works at all".

The bigger break came in mid-to-late September. [SE Ranking's own study](https://seranking.com/blog/unrelated-google-results-research-study/) found Google returning unrelated results for 57.3% of keywords in a 10,000-keyword research sample on September 17, up from 0.09% the day before. Those were research checks run apart from customer projects, and 96.5% of the unrelated blocks began at position 9 or lower. The study cites Nozzle and Keyword.com measuring the same kind of "poisoned" results.

In an [r/DoSEO thread on SE Ranking's tracking accuracy](https://www.reddit.com/r/DoSEO/comments/1wt27us/se_ranking_tracking_accuracy_issues/), the starter reported that "95% keyword positions have just disappeared while ranking on Google". A commenter who says they co-founded another rank tracker replied: "All the tools are in the same boat."

On October 2, the SE Ranking team confirmed "an issue affecting rank checks following the recent Google spam update" and said it was correcting "the affected historical data retroactively". Its study says it "corrected about 95% of historical data within two weeks of opening the incident, then completed the correction", and added filtering and automatic rechecks.

The thread starter reported "SER is working again" on October 20. The study says Google's results turned unstable again at the end of October, and on November 11 a user reported "Massive drop offs or empty data" while "manual checks show all terms ranking top".

We still score reliability 2.5: users had weeks of unreliable reports, they say support gave no timeline before the October confirmation, and the problem came back.

The cheapest plan roughly doubled this year. An [archived copy of the pricing page from January 31, 2026](https://web.archive.org/web/20260131065848/https://seranking.com/subscription.html) shows Essential at $65 a month for 5 projects and 500 daily keywords, and Pro at $119 for 30 projects, 3 seats and 2,000 keywords. By February 28, Core had replaced them at $129.

Core also added local and content tools, API credits and AI prompts that Essential lacked. Still, an Essential user now pays double, and a Pro user pays a little more for the same keywords with a third of the projects and a single seat. Trustpilot reviewers in April and June say they were moved to new plans without being asked, one at a price "five times more expensive than the one I had (for the same features)".

API access does not require a full plan. The [API page](https://seranking.com/api.html) offers pay-as-you-go from $50 per 250K credits, the pricing page lists a standalone API plan from $179 a month billed annually, and the add-on stacks on Core or Growth. SE Ranking's pages quote different API prices (its API FAQ says the add-on "starts at $149/month"), so confirm the figure at checkout.

For white-label reporting, an agency pays for Core or Growth plus the Agency Pack at $69 a month, sold on annual billing only. On November 28, SE Ranking's Black Friday banner offered an extra 20% off annual plans until December 4.

## AI Visibility Tracking: 25 Prompts, Then SE Visible

Core and Growth include AI visibility tracking through SE Visible: 25 prompts a day across 5 AI engines. SE Ranking's pages disagree on which five: the pricing card names AI Overviews, AI Mode, Gemini, ChatGPT and Copilot "and more", while [its data page](https://seranking.com/our-data.html) lists Perplexity instead of Copilot, so confirm the engines you need. The allowance used to be bigger. The February 28 archive shows 100 prompts on Core and 250 on Growth, and [PricingSaaS's log of SE Ranking pricing changes](https://pricingsaas.com/companies/seranking/diffs/2026W45) recorded the cut to 25 between its October 26 and November 2 captures. For scale, Ahrefs Lite includes 150 AI prompt checks a month and Mangools Basic bundles 500 monitored prompts.

Perplexity is gone too. SE Ranking says it could refresh few Perplexity results after late September and disabled it for new accounts, because Perplexity's "terms of service prohibit automated queries and data collection"; PricingSaaS logged Moz removing Perplexity on October 26. One buyer paid for SE Visible because the pricing page still listed it, and SE Ranking's reply admitted "that listing was our mistake".

The next step up is a separate [SE Visible plan](https://visible.seranking.com/pricing/). SE Visible starts at $89 a month for 75 tracked prompts, and Claude tracking starts at the $399 Agency tier.

## Support, Refunds and Cancellation

SE Ranking's policy allows refunds only when the service fails, and reviewers say they were refused even then. Its pricing FAQ says: "You can cancel anytime. Payments are non-refundable, except in cases where the service wasn't delivered as promised (or where a special promotion specifies otherwise)." Strict terms are common in this category: [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/pricing) says it "does not issue refunds in general", and our own fees are non-refundable too, while Mangools offers a "48-hour money back guarantee".

During the autumn outage, one Trustpilot reviewer says they asked for a credit after a week in which client reports could not run, and were told: "No. We are not giving refunds." A Capterra reviewer in November 2025 says SE Ranking's answer was "to grant a few extra days on the end of our current subscription."

Live chat runs 24/5, per SE Ranking's own replies, and Growth adds dedicated support. Outside incidents, reviewers rate it well: Capterra users give customer service 4.7, and SE Ranking has "Replied to 52% of negative reviews" on Trustpilot. During the September incident, one reviewer found it "almost impossible to actually speak to someone beyond the AI support bots."

## Data Depth and Coverage

SE Ranking's [FAQ](https://seranking.com/faq.html) says its backlink index and monthly keyword refreshes "are on par with Ahrefs and Semrush", and concedes that on search volume "SE Ranking's figures currently trail both competitors". Its pages also differ on size: [its data page](https://seranking.com/our-data.html) says "2.7T backlinks", while [its backlink checker page](https://seranking.com/backlink-checker.html) says "2.9 trillion backlinks". The data page's country table lists 5.5B keywords, 594M of them in the US.

Coverage can thin in small markets: SEO consultant [Corina Burri reports](https://corinaburri.com/se-ranking-review/) bulk keyword checks for German-speaking Switzerland often return "a red line with no data", though opening a single keyword shows its data. Its [backlink gap tool](https://seranking.com/backlink-gap-analyzer.html) compares you with up to five competitors. A Capterra reviewer summed up the trade-off in 2023: "The keyword research data is just not at the same scale as big players like Ahrefs." Our [Semrush vs Ahrefs breakdown](https://geotoolbox.ai/blog/semrush-vs-ahrefs) covers where each one is stronger.

## How to Test SE Ranking Before You Pay

Use the 14-day trial to check what matters most to you:

1. Track 20 keywords you can check by hand, and compare them with Search Console for a week.
2. Build one client report and send it to yourself. The trial opens the Agency Pack, so test white label now: after the trial it is annual only.
3. Open a support ticket on a weekday and on a weekend, and time the answers.
4. Price your real setup: plan, Agency Pack, extra seats and any SE Visible plan.

If it passes, start the base plan on monthly billing; only the Agency Pack forces an annual commitment.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is SE Ranking legit?

Yes. SE Ranking is an established SEO software company, rated 4.8 on G2 from 1,495 reviews and 4.1 on Trustpilot from 722. Our concerns are about its 2026 reliability and pricing changes, not its legitimacy.

### Does SE Ranking have a free plan or free trial?

There is no free subscription plan, but Core and Growth both come with a 14-day free trial and no credit card, and its [rank tracker page](https://seranking.com/position-tracking.html) checks 5 keywords a day for free. The trial includes up to 10 projects, 750 tracked keywords a day and 20 AI prompts a day.

### Is SE Ranking better than Semrush?

For tracking many keywords on a budget, yes: Core tracks 2k keywords a day for $129 a month, against 500 on Semrush's $139.95 SEO plan. For a broader marketing suite, Semrush is stronger.
