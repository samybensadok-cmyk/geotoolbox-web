## Terminology decisions the data settles

1. **`KI-` prefix, never `AI-` for German concepts.** `ki sichtbarkeit` 140/350 vs
   `ai sichtbarkeit` 10. Not close. → KI-Sichtbarkeit, KI-Suche, KI-Antworten, KI-Engines.
2. **`AI Overviews` stays English.** 1,900 vs 880 for Google's own German label
   „Übersicht mit KI". Same for **`Google AI Mode`** (1,600, English). Do not translate either.
3. **`Share of Voice` stays.** 390 vs zero for „Share of Model" (which DE trade blogs use but
   nobody searches).
4. **`GEO` / `Generative Engine Optimization` is the volume head** and is used untranslated in
   German — including inside Google's own AI Overview for `ki sichtbarkeit`. Keep it English,
   expand it once per page.
5. **`KI-Sichtbarkeit` is the positioning term**, not the volume term: KD 0, +143 % YoY, and it
   is what every ranking page, every AI-Overview citation and Google's own summary calls the
   concept. It belongs in H1s and meta titles even though its raw volume is small.

## OPEN QUESTION for the Fable native-fluency QA pass
**„Zitate" or „Zitationen" for an AI citation?** `Zitat` = a quoted passage; `Zitation` = the act
of citing a source — technically closer to what we mean. Ahrefs DE (the most professionally
localized direct competitor) ships **„Zitate"**; Google's AI Overview and gerlach.media use
**„Zitationen"**. Volume is uninformative (both ~170 and both polluted: `ki zitate` = AI-generated
quotations, `zitationen` = academic citation). Fable adjudicates; whichever wins goes in the
glossary and is applied corpus-wide in ONE sweep.

## Meta-title targeting (applies to the post-merge keyword pass)
Standing rule: **no brand suffix in any title tag**, ≤ ~60 chars.
- **Home** — lead `KI-Sichtbarkeit`, support with the engines. Do not lead with GEO alone.
- **/de/features** — the tool-category framing (`KI-Sichtbarkeit`, `GEO-Tools`).
- **/de/pricing** — `Preise` + `KI-Sichtbarkeit` + the euro entry price.
- **Feature pages** — each takes its own concept term (`KI-Sichtbarkeit`, `KI-Crawler`,
  `Wettbewerber`, `Zitate/Zitationen`), never a literal translation of the EN head term.
