# Service and pricing CRO redesign — 6 October 2026

## Research scope
English-language query snapshot: “AI SEO services”, “generative engine optimization agency”, “generative engine optimization services”, “AI visibility tools”, plus named competitor queries. Search results were mixed-language and location was not controlled. These are search-visible/category benchmarks, not a verified Google top-10 ranking report. No competitor conversion analytics were available. Patterns below are hypotheses, not evidence of conversion lift.

| Benchmark | Observed pattern | GeoToolbox decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [WebFX AI search services](https://www.webfx.com/seo/services/ai-search-optimization/) | Public starting price, named deliverables, proposal CTA and proprietary tracking product | Bring engagement pricing forward; explain tangible outputs. Preserve the founder-led differentiation. |
| [Ignite Visibility AI SEO](https://ignitevisibility.com/services/ai-seo/) | Outcome-led heading, proposal CTA, early results and links to case studies | Put concise proof near the top, with source context. Avoid repeating the proof narrative. |
| [Peec pricing](https://peec.ai/pricing) | Audience fit and prompts/models/frequency visible in plan comparison; additional models separated | Keep audience switching; foreground usage and explain what costs extra. |
| [Otterly pricing](https://otterly.ai/pricing) | Short persona descriptions; prompt and engine limits; add-ons explicitly identified | Make limits easy to compare and keep qualification beside the purchase action. |

## Decisions
- Reuse the new homepage's dark opening, teal, typography and restrained borders.
- Give each service a distinct promise, deliverables and proof context; preserve route and canonical intent.
- Three starting options first; advanced retainers and add-ons use native expandable sections.
- Preserve all prices, commitments, report credit terms, direct checkout routes and appointment destinations.
- Source and date all site performance figures. Do not blend Google impressions with Bing citation appearances. Own-site evidence is not a client outcome or a guarantee.
- Preserve the full supporting proof in expandable content, rather than repeat it in several open sections.
- Pricing retains its localized copy, audience segmentation, annual billing, currency and promotion routing.
- Bring monthly credits to the cards from the existing comparison data. Explain trial restrictions near the CTA.
- Retain crawlable FAQ content, schema and internal service links. No traffic-based decisions to remove routes.

## Measurement after release
Capture a baseline before rollout and compare by landing page, device and acquisition source. Primary outcomes: qualified booked calls / service landing sessions; paid diagnostic purchases / service landing sessions; completed trial signups / pricing sessions; trial-to-paid conversion. CTA clicks are diagnostic only. Use completed booking and payment records for outcomes. Keep existing analytics intact; no new consent-dependent tracking is introduced by this patch.

Check Search Console impressions, clicks and query mix for each service URL. Do not attribute a before/after change solely to design. Use a controlled test where traffic permits; otherwise label results directional. No claimed CRO uplift before live measurement.
